# Sample DSRC — Acceptable Use

> Training sample — a simplified, fictional excerpt modeled on HPCMP DSRC policy. Illustrative only; not authoritative. Effective 2026-01-01. Document ID: AUP-2026.

## Accounts and authentication
Access requires an approved account requested through pIE (Portal to the Information Environment) and authentication with Kerberos and your YubiKey (or CAC) plus PIN. You are responsible for your account, YubiKey, and PIN; sharing them with anyone violates the user agreement and is grounds for suspension.

## Login nodes
Login nodes are shared and are for editing, compiling, file management, and job submission only. Do not run production or memory- or CPU-intensive work on them — such processes may be terminated to protect other users.

## Prohibited use
The following are not permitted on any center system: cryptocurrency mining, unauthorized scanning or penetration testing, storing personal (non-work) data, and bypassing the scheduler or documented resource limits.

## Enforcement
Suspected violations are logged and reviewed. Report security concerns to the HPC Help Desk. Repeat or serious violations result in loss of access and referral to your sponsoring organization.
